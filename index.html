The dilemma

Courses that promise shortcuts. Newsletters optimized for clicks. Gurus who peaked in 2018 and have been selling that story ever since. The internet is full of people telling you how to build a business they never actually built.

 

You've been through enough of it to know the difference. You're not looking for motivation. You're not looking for a 5-step framework. You're looking for honest thinking from someone who's still in it — and a room of peers who hold you to a higher standard.

 

That's what The Board is.

The man behind it

I built this because I needed it.

Soren Hale

Soren Hale

Soren Hale

I've been building businesses for over a decade. Some worked. Some didn't. Along the way I realized that the hardest part was never the strategy, it was finding people who understood the game at the same level.


Not mentors. Not coaches. Peers.


People who'd made the same mistakes, asked the same questions, and were still showing up every morning because they genuinely love building things. That group was impossible to find online. So I built it.


The Board is what I wish had existed when I was figuring things out. It's not a course. It's not a coaching program. It's a room, and the people in it make it worth being part of.
